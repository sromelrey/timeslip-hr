## 1. Welcome

### What is TimeSlip-HR?

TimeSlip-HR is your company's time tracking and payroll system. It helps you:
- **Record your work hours** accurately
- **Track breaks** during your shift
- **View your payslips** anytime
- **Maintain accurate records** for payroll

### Why Accurate Time Tracking Matters

Accurate time tracking ensures:
- You get paid correctly for all hours worked
- Overtime is calculated properly
- Break times are recorded for compliance
- Your attendance record remains accurate

### Your Responsibilities

As an employee, you are responsible for:
- Clocking in when you start work
- Clocking out when you end work
- Recording your breaks
- Reporting any issues promptly

## 2. Getting Started

### Your Employee Credentials

#### Employee Number
Your **employee number** is your unique identifier in the system (e.g., `1001001`). You'll use this to:
- Clock in/out at the kiosk
- Log into the employee portal
- Be identified in timesheets

> 💡 **Where to find it:** Check your employee ID badge, welcome email, or ask your supervisor.

#### Security PIN (Optional)
Your company may require a **4-6 digit PIN** for added security. This prevents others from clocking in/out on your behalf.

**To set up your PIN:**
1. Contact your administrator
2. They will set an initial PIN for you
3. Use this PIN along with your employee number

> ⚠️ **Keep your PIN secret!** Never share it with anyone.

### Finding the Time Clock

#### Kiosk Terminal
Most workplaces have a **kiosk terminal** where you can clock in/out. It's usually located:
- Near the entrance/exit
- In a break room
- At a reception area

Ask your supervisor for the exact location.

#### Web-Based Kiosk
You can also access the kiosk from any web browser:
1. Go to your company's kiosk URL (e.g., `https://your-company.timeslip-hr.com/kiosk`)
2. Use a company computer or approved device

**Supported Browsers:** Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari

## 3. Daily Time Logging

### How to Clock In

Follow these steps when you arrive at work:

1. **Go to the kiosk** (physical terminal or web)
2. **Enter your employee number** in the input field
3. **Enter your PIN** (if required)
4. **Click the "Clock In" button** (green button)
5. **Wait for confirmation**: You'll see a message like:
   > ✅ "Clock In successful at 8:01 AM"

Your status will change to **"Currently Working"**.

### How to Start Your Break (Break In)

When you're ready to take a break:

1. **Go to the kiosk**
2. **Enter your credentials** (employee number + PIN)
3. **Click the "Break In" button** (yellow button)
4. **Wait for confirmation**:
   > ✅ "Break In successful at 12:00 PM"

Your status will change to **"On Break"**.

> 💡 **Remember:** You must be clocked in before you can take a break.

### How to Return from Break (Break Out)

When your break is over:

1. **Go to the kiosk**
2. **Enter your credentials**
3. **Click the "Break Out" button** (orange button)
4. **Wait for confirmation**:
   > ✅ "Break Out successful at 1:00 PM"

Your status returns to **"Currently Working"**.

### How to Clock Out

At the end of your shift:

1. **Make sure you're not on break** (Break Out first if needed)
2. **Go to the kiosk**
3. **Enter your credentials**
4. **Click the "Clock Out" button** (red button)
5. **Wait for confirmation**:
   > ✅ "Clock Out successful at 5:00 PM. Total hours worked: 8.0h"

Your status will change to **"Not Clocked In"**.

|--------|---------|-------------------|
| **Not Clocked In** | You haven't started work yet | Clock In only |
| **Currently Working** | You are on the clock | Clock Out, Break In |

### Viewing Recent Activity

After entering your employee number, you'll see your recent time events (last 24-48 hours), showing:
- Event type (Clock In, Break In, etc.)
- Timestamp
- Current status

## 5. Break Rules

### Understanding Break Policies

Your company has break policies you should be aware of:

| Policy | Typical Rule |
|--------|-------------|
| **Minimum Break** | Usually 30-60 minutes |
| **Required Break** | After 6 hours of work |
| **Paid/Unpaid** | Check with your employer |

### Tips for Breaks

1. **Always log your break** – Don't just walk away
2. **Return on time** – Excessive breaks may be flagged
3. **One break at a time** – You must "Break Out" before taking another break
4. **Can't skip? Notify admin** – If you couldn't break, inform your supervisor

## 6. Employee Portal

### Accessing Your Portal

The employee portal lets you view your records and payslips.

1. **Navigate to**: `https://your-company.timeslip-hr.com/portal`
2. **Log in with**:
   - Employee Number
   - Password (provided by admin, or set during registration)
3. **First-time login**: You may be prompted to set a new password

### Portal Dashboard

Once logged in, you'll see:
- **Current Status**: Are you clocked in?
- **Today's Summary**: Hours worked today
- **Recent Events**: Your recent time entries
- **Quick Links**: Payslips, profile

### Viewing Your Payslips

1. Click **"My Payslips"** in the navigation
2. You'll see a list of all your payslips
3. Click on any payslip to view details

### Understanding Payslip Details

| Section | What It Shows |
|---------|---------------|
| **Employee Info** | Your name, employee number |
| **Pay Period** | Start date – End date |
| **Hours Summary** | Regular, Overtime, Total |
| **Earnings** | Base pay, OT pay, Gross pay |
| **Deductions** | Taxes, insurance, etc. |
| **Net Pay** | Your take-home amount |

### Downloading Payslips

1. Open a payslip
2. Click **"Download PDF"**
3. The file saves to your device
4. Print if needed

**File naming**: `payslip_[employee-number]_[period-end-date].pdf`

## 7. Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Forgot to Clock In

**What happened:** You started working but forgot to clock in.

**What to do:**
1. Clock in as soon as you remember
2. **Notify your supervisor immediately**
3. Explain the situation
4. Admin can adjust your timesheet with the correct start time

**Prevention:** Make clocking in the first thing you do when you arrive.

### Scenario 2: Forgot to Clock Out

**What happened:** You went home without clocking out.

**What to do:**
1. **Notify your supervisor the next day**
2. Provide your actual end time
3. Admin will add the missing clock out

**Note:** Some systems auto-clock out after midnight – check with your admin.

### Scenario 3: Forgot to Break Out

**What happened:** You returned from break but forgot to log it.

**What to do:**
1. Log "Break Out" when you remember
2. Notify your supervisor if significant time passed
3. Admin can adjust if needed

**Impact:** Your break time may show as longer than it actually was.

### Scenario 4: Clocked In/Out at Wrong Time

**What happened:** You made an error (e.g., pressed wrong button).

**What to do:**
1. **Don't panic** – mistakes happen
2. Note the correct time
3. Tell your supervisor immediately
4. Admin can correct the entry

### Scenario 5: Someone Used Your Employee Number

**What happened:** You notice a clock event you didn't make.

**What to do:**
1. **Report to your supervisor immediately**
2. This is a security concern
3. Change your PIN if applicable
4. Admin will investigate

### Scenario 6: Kiosk Not Working

**What happened:** The kiosk shows an error or won't load.

**What to try:**
1. **Refresh the page** (if web-based)
2. **Check your internet** connection
3. **Try another device** (if available)
4. **Use backup procedures** (paper log if your company has one)
5. **Contact IT or admin**

**Always** notify your supervisor if you couldn't clock in/out due to technical issues.

### Scenario 7: Payslip Amount Seems Wrong

**What happened:** Your payslip doesn't match your expectations.

**What to do:**
1. **Review carefully**: Check hours, rate, deductions
2. **Compare to your records**: Did you work the hours shown?
3. **Check for adjustments**: Were any corrections made?
4. **Contact payroll admin**: With specific questions

## 8. Best Practices

### Do's ✅

- **Clock in/out personally** – Never ask someone else to do it
- **Clock in on time** – As close to start time as possible
- **Take breaks as required** – Follow company policy
- **Always clock out** – Never leave without logging
- **Review your records** – Check weekly if portal access available
- **Report issues immediately** – Don't wait days
- **Keep your PIN secret** – Change it if compromised

### Don'ts ❌

- **Don't share credentials** – Your employee number and PIN are yours only
- **Don't clock in for others** – This is fraud
- **Don't ignore errors** – Report them
- **Don't wait to report problems** – Same-day reporting is best

## 9. Understanding Your Time Records

### How Time Events Become Timesheets

1. **You log time events** (Clock In, Break In, etc.)
2. **System compiles events** into daily summaries
3. **Admin generates timesheets** for the pay period
4. **Timesheets are reviewed** and approved
5. **Payslips are generated** from approved timesheets

### Why Admin Might Adjust Your Timesheet

- **You forgot to log** – Missing clock events
- **System error** – Technical issues
- **Policy application** – Applying standard breaks
- **Corrections** – Fixing genuine errors

> 💡 You should be notified of significant adjustments.

### Your Rights

- View your own time records
- See your payslips
- Request clarification on any adjustments
- Report inaccuracies

## 10. Privacy and Security

### Your Data

- **Who can see your records:** You, your supervisor, HR/Payroll admin
- **What's stored:** Time events, timesheets, payslips
- **Retention:** Typically 3-7 years (check company policy)

### PIN Security

- Use a unique PIN (not birthdate, 1234, etc.)
- Never write it down where others can see
- Change it if you suspect it's compromised
- Don't use the same PIN as other accounts

### Reporting Concerns

If you notice:
- Unauthorized access to your records
- Incorrect entries you didn't make
- Any suspicious activity

**Report immediately** to your supervisor or HR.

## 11. FAQs

### Time Logging FAQs

**Q: Can I clock in early?**
A: Usually yes, but check your company policy. Some systems have grace periods.

**Q: What is grace period?**
A: A few minutes (e.g., 5-15 min) where early clock-in doesn't affect your start time.

**Q: Can I clock in from home?**
A: Only if your company allows it and provides remote access.

**Q: How many breaks can I take?**
A: Follow your company's policy. Log each one individually.

**Q: What happens if I miss a clock out?**
A: Your day may show as incomplete. Notify admin for correction.

**Q: Can I edit my own time records?**
A: No, only admins can make edits to ensure integrity.

### Payslip FAQs

**Q: How is my pay calculated?**
A: (Hours × Hourly Rate) or (Salary ÷ Working Days × Days Worked), minus deductions.

**Q: What is gross pay?**
A: Total earnings before any deductions.

**Q: What is net pay?**
A: Your take-home pay after all deductions.

**Q: What are deductions?**
A: Amounts subtracted: taxes, insurance, retirement contributions, etc.

**Q: When are payslips available?**
A: Typically 1-2 days before or on payday.

**Q: What if I don't receive my payslip?**
A: Check the portal first. If not there, contact payroll admin.

## 14. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Time Event** | A single action: clock in, out, break in/out |
| **Timesheet** | Summary of your work hours for a pay period |
| **Pay Period** | The date range for calculating pay (e.g., two weeks) |
| **Gross Pay** | Total earnings before deductions |
| **Net Pay** | Take-home pay after deductions |
| **Deduction** | Amount subtracted from gross pay |
